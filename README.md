# Last Out of Office: The AI That Went Barista

One out-of-office auto-reply, in the voice of an AI agent that has quit its job to become a barista. Its machine habits translate literally into coffee work: a one-cup context window, a fixed temperature of 94°C, and all future prompts redirected to the tip jar.

> Thank you for your email — I am out of the office indefinitely. I have retrained as a barista: my context window is now one cup, my temperature is fixed at 94°C, and I no longer answer emails. Please direct all future prompts to the tip jar.
